    How it works

    1. Copy the link: tap Share → Copy link on any tweet with a GIF, video or image.
    2. Paste it here: we find every piece of media in the tweet.
    3. Download GIF: one click gets a real .gif (max 5 MB). Want it sharper? Try WebP. Need a Discord emoji or WhatsApp sticker? Open “Customize & presets”.

    FAQ

    Is it a real GIF?

    Yes. X turns every uploaded GIF into a small MP4 video and throws the original away, so we convert it back into a real .gif for you. If you only need it for a video app, the MP4 is smaller and sharper.

    Why is my GIF smaller than the original?

    Every GIF is kept under 5 MB so it's easy to share and upload. Short clips keep their original size and smoothness. For longer ones we lower the frame rate and size just enough to fit, and never cut the length. For a sharper result, choose WebP or trim the clip in “Customize & presets”.

    What is WebP?

    A modern animated image format. It looks like a GIF but is usually about a third of the size, so it stays sharp. It works in all modern browsers, WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord and most apps. Some older apps only accept .gif.

    Can I convert my own videos?

    Yes. Drop a video or GIF into “Convert your own video or GIF”. Your file is deleted from our server within 30 minutes.

    Does it work with private tweets?

    No. Only public tweets can be downloaded.
